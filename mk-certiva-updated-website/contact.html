FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does ISO certification take?

Most projects take 3–6 months depending on organization size, current systems maturity and readiness of documentation.

Do you handle documentation for us?

Yes. We support manuals, SOPs, procedures, HACCP plans, risk assessments and related documentation.

What is the difference between ISO 22000 and FSSC 22000?

We help assess your organization and explain the applicable requirements, scope and implementation path for each scheme.

Do you provide training?

Yes. Training includes food safety, ISO standards, internal auditor, HACCP, GMP/GHP, BRCGS and leadership topics.

Can you help with BRCGS or GlobalG.A.P.?

Yes. We provide consultancy, documentation, implementation and audit-readiness support for these standards.

Do you offer post-certification support?

Yes. We provide continual improvement, compliance and audit-readiness support beyond certification.
